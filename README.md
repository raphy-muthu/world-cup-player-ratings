# World Cup 2026 Player Ratings

## Problem
After watching this year's FIFA World Cup, I was intrigued by how a player's performance between club and country can fluctuate. Some of the worlds biggest stars brought their skills to the tournament, while others struggled to perform. On the other hand, some relatively unknown and under the radar players became stars of the tournament, not only by scoring goals, but by lifting up their team as well. I wanted to properly reflect the performances of players in this World Cup relative to how they perform for their club. However, I did not simply want to go based on traditional stats (goals, assists, etc.). 

I wanted to reflect the actual impact of a player, which must account for many subtle factors (age, height, position, etc). By doing this, I was able to expand my predictions to include midfielders and defenders rather than simply strikers.
